Artificial intelligence is the simulation of human intelligence in machines that are programmed to think like humans and mimic their actions.

Machine learning is a subset of AI that allows systems to learn and improve from experience without being explicitly programmed.

Deep learning, a branch of machine learning, uses neural networks with many layers to analyze complex patterns.

LangGraph is a framework that enables developers to create LLM-based workflows using state graphs.

Gemini by Google is a family of multimodal large language models capable of understanding text, code, images, and more.
